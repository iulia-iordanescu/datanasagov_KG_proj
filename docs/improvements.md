- **Other catalogs:** some things fit only data.nasa.gov. The catalog's address is fixed in the code (`010_harvest/010_harvest_helpers/ckan_client.py`); "undefined" is treated as a maintainer (`020_clean/020_clean_helpers/maintainers.py`); splitting into strata needs a maintainer field that other catalogs may not fill; step 040's prompts say "a catalog of NASA datasets". From: the methods review of 2026-10-11, item 11.
- **Competency questions:** 10 to 20 questions the knowledge graph should answer, to judge it by. From: the literature review of 2026-10-07; the method is Grüninger & Fox (1995), "Methodology for the Design and Evaluation of Ontologies", IJCAI-95 Workshop on Basic Ontological Issues in Knowledge Sharing.
- **An entity class hierarchy for the schemas:** the ground truth schema is flat (no entity class is a kind of another). An umbrella entity class with subclasses is standard in knowledge graph schemas (subClassOf in W3C, "RDF Schema 1.1", Recommendation, 25 February 2014, [page](https://www.w3.org/TR/rdf-schema/)); for people and groups it has a standard name, Agent: FOAF puts Person, Organization and Group under it (Brickley and Miller, "FOAF Vocabulary Specification 0.99", 14 January 2014, [page](http://xmlns.com/foaf/spec/)), and PROV-O puts Person, Organization and SoftwareAgent under it (W3C, "PROV-O: The PROV Ontology", Recommendation, 30 April 2013, [page](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/)). With it, a pin could say any -> Agent instead of listing Organization, Person and Team (DEVELOPED_BY_PARTY, SPONSORED_BY_PARTY), and the graph could answer questions across the kinds. It touches the schema reader and its file format, the pin check, step 040's induced schema, and step 070's translation table, so it is a design change for after the first run. Some entity classes only ever appear as the object of one predicate, and their definitions just restate it: Purpose (HAS_PURPOSE), Task (RESPONSIBLE_FOR), Role (SERVES_AS), ProcessingLevel (HAS_PROCESSING_LEVEL), SpatialRepresentation (HAS_SPATIAL_REPRESENTATION). They cause no disagreement today, unlike Value and Time, which were merged for that reason on 2026-10-20; with a hierarchy they could sit under one umbrella entity class (e.g. Label). Evidence that an induced schema is often finer than one written by people, so that a hierarchy would let the two meet: in TRACE-KG (Abolhasani et al., arXiv:2604.03496v3), 48.1% of the entity classes of their benchmark's hand-built schemas (weighted by use, all scopes together) were matched only by a narrower entity class of the induced schema, against 50.2% matched by an equivalent one (Table 2, *Combined*), and of its accepted matches for entity classes, 13.8% were best at the coarsest level of its hierarchy (appendix C.2, Table 6). From: 2026-10-20; TRACE-KG (Abolhasani et al., arXiv:2604.03496v3), for the evidence.
- **A number cut from its unit in a lookup:** a letter next to a digit counts as a break between words (so "67P" is found in "Comet67P"), so "1 km" is also found in a text that says "1 km2" (an area), and the check of a subject instance or object instance against the record's text (`common/common_helpers/text_match.py`, `Text.contains`) raises no flag for it. Pairing is not affected: "1 km" and "1 km2" never pair. From: 2026-10-24.
- **Ideas from TRACE-KG not to adopt, and why** (Abolhasani et al., arXiv:2604.03496v3):
  - *Prefer recall over precision*, inferred facts included: its extraction prompts say "Prefer recall over precision" (`TKG_Prompts.py`, lines 28 and 883) and keep a fact rated "Plausible inference; partial support (still include)" (line 77). That conflicts with guidelines 1 and 7 of *What counts as a fact*, "Only what the record states" and "Not uncertain facts".
  - Subject instances and object instances in the model's words: its prompt has the model name each thing in a short, reusable form, which may not occur in the text as written (line 865). That conflicts with rule 1 of *Writing a classed triple* (the record's own words, as written) and with the source text check (`common/common_helpers/validate.py`).
  - A qualifier dictionary on each edge (section 4.4, appendix B.5): there, a "qualifier" is a condition a triple holds under (when, where, how certain), kept with the edge, not a word inside a subject or object instance as in this project (see *qualifier* in `terminology.md`). Its "UncertaintyQualifier" keeps hedged facts, against guideline 7, "Not uncertain facts". Their ablation found qualifiers mattered most on their benchmark, judged by a model (appendix C.4).
  - Never more than one edge between the same subject and object instances: its prompt says to merge them all (line 1211). That contradicts its own section 4.5, which merges only two edges between the same things that mean the same, and would drop distinct facts. Suppose a record says NASA developed and sponsored a mission; then DEVELOPED_BY_PARTY and SPONSORED_BY_PARTY (ground truth schema) join the same two instances, and one would be lost.
  - Its "Leakage" metric, the overlap of 4-word runs between the text and the names of things (section 5.1, appendix C.1), counts copying the record's words as a fault: the opposite of rule 1 of *Writing a classed triple*.
  - A model judge, as the evaluation, of whether a fact "can be supported or inferred" from the graph (`TKG_Experiment_1.py`, line 1359; its section 5.1 says the judge allows "no inferred edges"): inferred facts would count as found, and the judge's own errors go unmeasured. Step 070 compares with a ground truth a person checked.
  - A model's own confidence as a gate (appendices C.2 and D: below 0.88 goes to a person): the paper gives no evidence that it is calibrated. A person checks every row of the translation table instead.

## 040_induce_schema

- **Splitting a broad predicate by its object's entity class:** a stage after stage 4, checked by code. For each predicate whose patterns reach several object entity classes, the model may propose splits, each a new predicate with the object entity classes it takes; code checks that each object entity class named is among that predicate's patterns and goes to only one split, reassigns the triple instances by their pattern, and lists every split it rejects, with why. Suppose SOURCED_FROM (current schema) has patterns with a Mission object and with a Spacecraft object; then it would split into one predicate for each, which can translate to FROM_MISSION and FROM_SPACECRAFT (ground truth schema). This fixes the cause behind *Gaps in the translation table* (1), under 070, and makes *Translation that depends on the entity classes* a fallback for the predicates not split. Cost: a few paid calls in 040, then running 060 and 070 again. Risk: more predicates, so a longer schema text in every 060 prompt (see *Patterns in or out of the prompt*). From: TRACE-KG (Abolhasani, Ba, He & Pan, "Traceable Context-Enriched Knowledge Graph Generation with Induced Schemas", arXiv:2604.03496v3, 18 Oct 2026, [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03496v3)): its model may split an entity class into several (`split_class`, appendix B.4.2), and its code keeps only the split entity class's own members, each in one split only (`execute_split_class`, `TKG_Main.py`, line 4796); it sets each triple instance's predicate with its subject's and object's entity classes in view (section 4.5); and its code carries out only actions it has checked (section 4.6).
- **The sample of texts:** 10 maintainers × 15 texts is the documented baseline, not a design shown to be best. Taking only the 10 largest maintainers, it can never sample the records of the other 412 maintainers, which hold 8.4% of the catalog's records (040's guide, *Known limits*; the methods review of 2026-10-11, item 3). Compare designs with `helpers/compare_runs.py`, guided by the saturation curve in 040's report. From: [`findings.md`](findings.md), 2026-10-12.
- **`min_support`** above 1 (it is 1, which keeps everything found): try other values once runs can be compared. From: 040's guide, *Settings*.
- **Near-duplicate texts inflate support.** If a maintainer's 15 texts are nearly the same text, every schema entry found in them counts 15 times. Measure how alike each maintainer's texts are; then drop the near-duplicates (taking others from the same maintainer), or count that maintainer once. It matters once `min_support` is above 1. 040's report lists twins between the texts it learned from and the pool, not near-duplicates among its own texts. From: my old to-do list.
- **Nothing filters junk from stage 2:** e.g. "dataset" as a bare subject instance, or "VNP43D66 is the BSA" read as an "is a" statement, both gaining real support. A list of generic subject instances to drop would cost nothing. From: my old to-do list.
- **Show the source text when labeling (stage 3):** stage 3 labels a subject or object instance by the shortest triple instance it appears in (040's guide, *How it works*), which may not say what it is. Suppose the shortest triple instance with "Dawn" is "Dawn" – "carries" – "GRaND"; then Dawn could be a mission or a spacecraft, while a source text such as "the GRaND instrument aboard the Dawn spacecraft" says which (see *Mission or spacecraft, when they share a name*, under 050). Sending each instance's source text too makes the label prompts longer. From: TRACE-KG (Abolhasani et al., arXiv:2604.03496v3), whose prompt for entity classes gives the model, for each thing, 20 to 120 words of context as its "PRIMARY evidence for meaning" (`TKG_Prompts.py`, line 419); that context is the model's own words, while this would be the record's.
- **Alternatives to stage 4 (merging synonymous labels):** grouping by similarity, passing the chosen labels forward from batch to batch, or another approach entirely. From: my old to-do list.
- **Which text fields to learn from:** 040 uses `title` and `notes` and any field 020's `extra_text_fields` adds; which other fields help is untested. First candidate: the names of the files attached to a record (e.g. `…L3m.8D.SST4…nc`), which can say what a dataset holds when its notes describe several (2026-10-19, record #4). From: my old to-do list.
- **Ideas to compare the induced schema with:** the Purview glossary, Neo4j's capabilities, the five science domains of NASA's Science Mission Directorate (SMD) site, and the filters data.nasa.gov's search offers. From: my old to-do list.

## 070_evaluate

- **Translation that depends on the entity classes:** a row of the translation table translates a predicate one way only, whatever the entity classes of its subject and object (070's guide, *Known limits*, *One translation per component class*). Let a row translate a predicate of the current schema according to its subject's entity class, its object's entity class, or both: rows for (subject's entity class, predicate), (predicate, object's entity class), and (subject's entity class, predicate, object's entity class), besides the predicate alone as now. The most specific row that matches wins: all three over either pair, either pair over the predicate alone. When a (subject's entity class, predicate) row and a (predicate, object's entity class) row both match and disagree, 070 stops and names both rows, so a person adds the row with all three. These keys have the shape of the current schema's patterns (e.g. [Dataset, SOURCED_FROM, Mission]), so the rows can be listed from the patterns. Example: SOURCED_FROM (current schema) translates to (none) today; with rows, SOURCED_FROM with a Mission object would translate to FROM_MISSION, with a Spacecraft object to FROM_SPACECRAFT (ground truth schema), and otherwise to (none). In record 2705e0d1 (pool position 3), the extracted triple "Aquarius L3 Weekly Polar-Gridded Sea Surface Salinity V005" (Dataset) SOURCED_FROM "Aquarius/SAC-D" (Mission) is a true fact, counted extracted only; with the (SOURCED_FROM, Mission) row it would translate to FROM_MISSION, the predicate of the ground truth triple "Aquarius L3 Weekly Polar-Gridded Sea Surface Salinity V005" (Dataset) FROM_MISSION "Aquarius/Satélite de Aplicaciones Científicas (SAC-D)" (Mission). Changes 070, the translation table's format, and the annotation tool's Translation table view. From: 2026-10-24.
- **Raise With Mentors: checking a sample of the occurrences left extracted only,** to see how far precision and recall are understated ([`metrics/pairs.md`](../070_evaluate/metrics/pairs.md), *The same fact in different words*). It's an involved process: such an occurrence can be the same fact in different words as a ground truth triple left ground truth only, a repeat of a fact already paired, a fact the ground truth lacks, the result of a wrong translation, or actually wrong, and each means something different. - **Kinds of translation, not only "means the same":** a row of the translation table now says only that a component class of the current schema means the same as one of the ground truth schema, or `(none)`. TRACE-KG (Abolhasani, Ba, He & Pan, "Traceable Context-Enriched Knowledge Graph Generation with Induced Schemas", arXiv:2604.03496v3, 18 Oct 2026, [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03496)) judges each pair of a ground truth schema component class and a candidate from the schema it learns: "Judgements are Equivalent, Narrower, Broader, or Unrelated", and "Equivalent counts as exact recovery and Narrower as compatible refinement" (section 5.2). A model proposes and verifies: it takes the closest candidates for each ground truth schema component class and checks each pair with "a structured LLM verifier, with targeted human auditing of low-confidence cases" (section 5.2). The confidence is the verifier's own: the model reports one with each judgement, and a judgement below 0.88 goes to a person (appendices C.2 and D). Their code has no 0.88 threshold: `TKG_Experiment_2.py` (commit ebe14b0), `write_audit_subset` (line 4653), writes, for each of the two splits it reports, up to 12 predicates and 10 entity classes of the ground truth schema for a person to check, the most used ones that found no counterpart first, chosen without the confidence (`audit_n = 12`, line 4170). Consider recording the kind in each row, e.g. narrower: suppose the current schema has MOUNTED_ON_SATELLITE and the ground truth schema has ABOARD; then MOUNTED_ON_SATELLITE is more specific than ABOARD, and its row would say narrower. Then decide which kinds count as within reach (narrower, probably; broader, not without more). Consider also whether a model's confidence could pick which rows a person must check, alongside *A second person checking a sample of the translation table*. A broader row is the case *Translation that depends on the entity classes* handles: SOURCED_FROM (current schema) is broader than FROM_MISSION (ground truth schema), and its object's entity class picks the narrower meaning. From: 2026-10-24.

- **Coverage of the ground truth schema, as a second number next to the recall upper bound:** TRACE-KG (Abolhasani et al., arXiv:2604.03496v3, as above) reports coverage: the proportion of the ground truth schema's component classes that the ground truth triples use ("ontology [ground truth schema] relations [predicates] appearing in the gold [ground truth] triples together with their domain and range concepts [subject classes and object classes]", section 5.2) that have an equivalent counterpart in the schema it learns ("Exact"), or an equivalent or narrower one ("Compat"; its table fits that reading, its text doesn't spell it out). - **Joining subject instances and object instances that name the same thing into one node** beyond the lists: similar spelling plus shared neighbors (two spacecraft names spelled alike that carry the same instrument), grouped into connected components, the longest name kept; generic words ("Data", "Product", "Level") never count as similar. Which subject instances and object instances to join across records, and which to keep as one node per record: specific named things join (one MODIS node, one Aqua, one NASA), since they are important and specific. Generic descriptions don't: "L2 pixels" (record #4, pool position 4), "radiance data" and "a series of screens" (pool position 2) each mean that record's own data or method, so one shared "L2 pixels" node would wrongly connect unrelated datasets. Matching words alone can't tell the two kinds apart, so 080 needs a test for it: e.g. a proper name or an acronym against a common noun phrase, or a model's judgment checked on a sample. Organizations inside organizations: the ground truth keeps an organization's name whole, as written (e.g. "NASA Ocean Biology Processing Group (OBPG)", pool position 4), because "NASA" there says whose group it is, while in "NASA Technical Standards Program" (pool position 0) it is part of the name, and wording alone can't tell the two apart. So that node won't join a "Peter Minnett" node from another record; 080 should link the two (the team includes the person) rather than leave them unconnected. Same name, different things: NASA often gives a mission and its spacecraft the same name (Dawn, Rosetta, Aqua). A Mission node and a Spacecraft node with the same name must stay two nodes, never joined because the names match. They may be linked (the spacecraft belongs to the mission), but only from a record that states it. What never to join, from TRACE-KG's prompt for joining (`TKG_Prompts.py`, lines 247–250): a thing and the entity class it belongs to ("Aqua" and "satellite"), a kind and a narrower kind ("sea surface temperature" and "skin sea surface temperature"), and a quantity and a measurement of it ("sea surface temperature" and "a sea surface temperature of 25 °C").
