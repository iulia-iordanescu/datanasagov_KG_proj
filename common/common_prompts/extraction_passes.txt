Completeness comes first. Extract every fact the record states$scope,
unless a rule leaves it out: a fact that is missed is the costliest error,
because nothing later finds it.

Work in two passes, in this order:
1. Find every thing the record names: the dataset its title names, and
   every instrument, spacecraft, mission, algorithm, variable, input
   dataset, organization, person, place, or other thing it names. Don't
   write this list in your answer.
2. Take the things one at a time. For each, state every fact the record
   gives about it$scope. A fact about any of these things counts as much as
   a fact about the dataset the title names: "the Kestrel product LT2 is
   computed from channel 7" gives "LT2" computed from "channel 7", with
   "LT2" as the subject, not the record's dataset.
The rules still hold, rule 21 on general facts among them.
