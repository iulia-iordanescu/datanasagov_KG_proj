# A second opinion on the ground truth

Many [ground truth triples](../docs/terminology.md#4-ground-truth-and-samples) were first drafted by a [model](../docs/terminology.md#8-the-pipeline) (step 050) and corrected by me; the rest I wrote. The facts the drafting model missed are the facts I had to add alone, and a person correcting a model's draft tends to keep what is there ([`070_evaluate/metrics/recall.md`](../070_evaluate/metrics/recall.md), *Assumes and can't see*). The standard remedy is a second annotator from another source, whose annotation shows what the first one missed: Tan et al., "Revisiting DocRED – Addressing the False Negative Problem in Relation Extraction", EMNLP 2022 ([paper](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.580/)), re-annotated a benchmark and found its labels incomplete. Here the second annotator is another model, which gives a **second opinion**: it drafts every [finished record](../docs/terminology.md#4-ground-truth-and-samples) again, and each ground truth triple is matched against that fresh draft. One draft is noisy (a model doesn't answer the same way twice), so the second opinion drafts each record several times, 3 repeats by default, and combines them (*The repeats*, below).

```
py helpers/second_opinion.py                       draft every finished record again in 3 repeats (asks once before paying), match, report
py helpers/second_opinion.py --apply true          the same, and record what was found in annotations/second_opinion.csv,
                                                   and the draft's facts to review in annotations/second_opinion_candidates.csv
py helpers/second_opinion.py --repeats <n>         another number of repeats (1 to 20)
py helpers/second_opinion.py --ids <id>,<id>       only these finished records
py helpers/second_opinion.py --model <name>        another model (the default: gemini-4-argon)
```

The ground truth is never changed: the second opinion writes no [drafted batch](../docs/terminology.md#4-ground-truth-and-samples), and nothing in `annotations/` unless `--apply true`, which writes only `annotations/second_opinion.csv` and `annotations/second_opinion_candidates.csv` (below). Decided on 2026-11-01; the entry in [`docs/improvements.md`](../docs/improvements.md), section *050_annotate*, says why, and why 3 repeats.

## How it drafts

Exactly the way step 050 drafts, so the second opinion is a draft 050 could have made: 050's prompt (`050_annotate/050_annotate_prompts/draft.txt`, with the shared rules and reply format of `common/common_prompts/`), the [ground truth schema](../docs/terminology.md#3-schemas) as 050 shows it (the file, plus the [component classes](../docs/terminology.md#3-schemas) coined and the [patterns](../docs/terminology.md#3-schemas) used in the finished [tuning records](../docs/terminology.md#7-evaluating-extraction-step-070): `common/common_helpers/ground_truth.py`, `ground_truth_schema_view`), 050's parsing and checks (`common/common_helpers/extraction.py`, `build_rows`), and 050's settings `max_chars`, `workers` and `thinking` (medium). The code is 050's own, called from `helpers/second_opinion.py` (`050_annotate/050_annotate_helpers/records.py`, `read_inputs` and `items_for`; `draft.py`, `ask_model`; `moves.py`, `build_rows`), so the two can't drift apart. One thing differs: 050 skips a record already in the ground truth; the second opinion drafts only records that are finished there (`common/common_helpers/ground_truth.py`, the selection `EVALUABLE`: every part of the [pool](../docs/terminology.md#4-ground-truth-and-samples), held-out records included, and records outside the pool), in pool order, or those of them named by `--ids`.

It runs in the frame every [step](../docs/terminology.md#8-the-pipeline) runs in (`common/common_helpers/step.py`, `run_step`): a [run id](../docs/terminology.md#8-the-pipeline) (`second_opinion_<date>_<time>`), a [log](../docs/terminology.md#8-the-pipeline), a [report](../docs/terminology.md#8-the-pipeline) and a [manifest](../docs/terminology.md#8-the-pipeline) in `outputs/intermediate_results/second_opinion/`, which record the model, every [setting](../docs/terminology.md#8-the-pipeline), the exact model the service said answered, the settings each call was sent with, and the prompts by [fingerprint](../docs/terminology.md#8-the-pipeline). It asks before its first [paid call](../docs/terminology.md#8-the-pipeline), showing every note first: which records it drafts (by part) and in how many repeats, what was skipped and why (an id not in the catalog, a record not finished, one with no text), whether some ground truth triples were drafted by the very model asked (column `drafted_by`), whether LiteLLM's answer cache may make the repeats copies of one (below), and the ground truth's problems. Every answer is cached in its own `cache/`, so a rerun with the same repeats, `--apply true` among them, pays nothing.

**The model.** `gemini-4-argon` by default (Google's flagship, released 2026-09-30; usable through NASA's LiteLLM on 2026-11-01; `gemini-3.1-pro-preview` is usable too): another maker than 060's model (`claude-opus-5-5`), and a later model than 050's (`gemini-2.5-pro`), so a fact both of those miss still has a chance. Through LiteLLM it is sent no temperature (Google: "Remove `temperature`, `top_p`, and `top_k` from your requests. If provided, they are ignored", [Gemini 4 guide](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/latest-model)) and its thinking level as `reasoning_effort` (LiteLLM maps it to Gemini's `thinking_level`, [LiteLLM's Gemini page](https://docs.litellm.ai/docs/providers/gemini)); the answer limit is the pipeline's 32,000 tokens (`common/common_helpers/llm.py`, `MAX_ANSWER_TOKENS`; Google advises at least 32,768 for this model, so a cut-off answer is possible on a very long record, and the report says so). With `LITELLM_BYPASS_ANSWER_CACHE=1` in `.env`, LiteLLM's own answer cache is bypassed as in every step.

## The repeats

One draft is one answer of the model, and a model doesn't answer the same way twice: a fact it states on one answer it may leave out on the next, so a ground truth triple one draft misses may be a miss of that answer alone. So `--repeats N` (3 by default) drafts every record N times, as the repeats 1 to N of the recipe (the setting `repeat` of the steps that call a model: `docs/running_on_nasa_laptop.md`, *Repeats: how much the numbers vary on their own*), all in one run of the helper. Each repeat has answers of its own: from 2 on, the repeat is part of each answer's cache key (`common/common_helpers/llm.py`, `cache_identity`), so repeat 1 reuses the answers a second opinion of one repeat paid for, and repeats 2 to N are asked anew. With `LITELLM_BYPASS_ANSWER_CACHE=1` in `.env`, LiteLLM doesn't answer a repeat from its own cache, so the repeats are fresh answers; without it, the notes before paying say the repeats may be copies of one.

It asks once, before the first paid call of any repeat, for all of them: the plan shows N repeats × the text pieces = the model calls, how many of them are already in the cache, and how many are to pay for, repeat by repeat. A rerun with the same N pays nothing; one with more repeats pays only for the new repeats. A record whose model call fails in some repeats is matched on the repeats that drafted it (column `repeats`), and the warnings name the repeats that failed; a rerun asks only the failed text pieces again.

## How it matches

For each drafted record, its ground truth triples (the [describes triple](../docs/terminology.md#2-triples) left out: code writes it on both sides) are paired with the fresh draft's [classed triples](../docs/terminology.md#2-triples) by step 070's own pairing (`070_evaluate/070_evaluate_helpers/pairing.py`, `pair_triples`): the largest set of [exact pairs](../docs/terminology.md#7-evaluating-extraction-step-070), then of [partial pairs](../docs/terminology.md#7-evaluating-extraction-step-070), each triple with at most one partner, ties broken by the triples' contents ([`070_evaluate/metrics/pairs.md`](../070_evaluate/metrics/pairs.md)). No [translation table](../docs/terminology.md#7-evaluating-extraction-step-070): both sides use the ground truth schema. No [partial pair review](../docs/terminology.md#7-evaluating-extraction-step-070) applies: those verdicts are about 060's extracted triples. In each repeat, a ground truth triple is found when it has a partner (exact or partial); a draft triple with no partner is a fact the ground truth lacks, or one its rules leave out on purpose, or a wrong one.

The repeats combined: a ground truth triple is **found by the second opinion** when at least one repeat found it, and **found in none** otherwise; with it, `found_in_repeats` (in how many of the repeats that drafted the record, k of N) and `found_exact_in_repeats` (in how many exactly). The draft triples no ground truth triple paired with are the union over the repeats: one classed triple once (compared as step 070's exact pairs compare: `common/common_helpers/triples_io.py`, `classed_key`), with the number of repeats that proposed it (a repeat stating it twice counts once). Why *at least one repeat*: the question is whether a second annotator states the fact at all, and a fact the model states on one answer in three is one it can state; how often it does is in `found_in_repeats`, not in the group.

A ground truth triple found in none was stated by one annotator only. It is not thereby wrong: the fresh draft can miss a fact, or state it in words the pairing can't match (*When a pair isn't the same fact*, in `pairs.md`), or leave out what the [annotation guidelines](../docs/terminology.md#4-ground-truth-and-samples) ask for (a qualifier taken out of an instance, rule 19). What it does tell: step 070's recall on the triples found is recall on facts two annotators agree on; recall on the triples found in none is recall on facts only I (or the drafting model and I) stated.

## What it writes

In `outputs/intermediate_results/second_opinion/`:

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `second_opinion_triples.csv` | Every repeat's fresh draft, in a drafted batch's columns (`050_annotate/050_annotate.md`, *Outputs*) and the column `repeat` (1 to N), `drafted_by` naming the second opinion's model. Every drafted record's, held-out ones included: read the held-out records' rows only through the counts below. |
| `second_opinion_matches.csv` | One row per ground truth triple of each drafted record: `record_id`, `position`, `part`, `status` (`found`: found in at least one repeat; `not found`: found in none), `repeats` (the repeats that drafted the record), `found_in_repeats`, `found_exact_in_repeats`, `entity_classes_agree_in_repeats` (in how many repeats its pair was a [strict pair](../docs/terminology.md#7-evaluating-extraction-step-070)), the ground truth triple (`ground_truth_*`, with its `drafted_by`), and its partner (`draft_*`, with the draft's [source text](../docs/terminology.md#1-records-and-their-text), and `draft_repeat`: the first repeat that paired it exactly, else the first that paired it partially); then, for TUNING records only, one row per draft triple with no partner, the union over the repeats (`status` `draft only`, `proposed_in_repeats`, and `draft_repeat`, the first repeat that proposed it): the facts to review. |
| `second_opinion_details.json` | The run's record: run id, model, service, the settings as sent, the settings given, the repeats; each record's pool position, part, the repeats that drafted it and those that failed (with the errors), ground truth triples, how many were found in 0, 1, ..., N repeats, found in at least one repeat, found in none, draft only (over the repeats), and the items 050's checks removed, by repeat; the records skipped while choosing; the records left out of at least one repeat because a model call failed; what `--apply true` wrote. |
| `cache/`, `_manifest.json` | As for every step. |

The report (`outputs/reports/second_opinion_<date>_<time>.md`), section *Results*: a table per record (the repeats that drafted it; ground truth triples; found in at least one repeat, found in every repeat, found in none; draft only, over the repeats); *Found by the second opinion*, the counts by part of the pool and by the ground truth triples' `drafted_by` (a row a model drafted and I corrected, or a row I wrote), and how many ground truth triples were found in 0, 1, ..., N repeats; *The second opinion's facts the ground truth lacks (tuning records)*, every draft-only triple of the tuning records with the number of repeats that proposed it and its source text, grouped by record, for review; *Provenance recorded*, what `--apply true` wrote (both files, with how many rows still have no decision), or how to write it.

**Held-out records.** They are drafted too, so that step 070 can group their triples, and their found / found in none counts are in the table and the counts. Their draft-only triples are counted and nowhere listed, not in the report, nor in `second_opinion_matches.csv`, nor in `annotations/second_opinion_candidates.csv`: reviewing them would let the [held-out part](../docs/terminology.md#7-evaluating-extraction-step-070) shape the ground truth and the annotation guidelines (`annotations/guidelines.md`, *Before you start*). Read `second_opinion_triples.csv` with the same care.

## The provenance: `annotations/second_opinion.csv`

With `--apply true`, one row per ground truth triple of every record drafted this run and per model, keyed by the record's id and the classed triple as the ground truth writes it, and the model (compared as step 070's exact pairs compare: `common/common_helpers/triples_io.py`, `classed_key`):

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `id`, `subject`, `subject_class`, `predicate`, `object`, `object_class` | The ground truth triple: the key. Not a second home of the fact: the ground truth file stays the one place it is stated, and a row whose triple is no longer there (reworded, deleted, its record unfinished again) is stale. |
| `model` | The model whose repeats drafted the record. |
| `repeats` | How many of its repeats drafted the record: N, fewer when a model call failed in some. |
| `found_in_repeats` | In how many of those repeats its draft paired with this triple (an exact or partial pair). |
| `found_exact_in_repeats` | In how many of them the pair was exact. |

Applying with a model rewrites that model's rows on the records drafted now (each replaced by this run's counts), keeps every other model's rows, drops a row of those records whose triple is no longer in the ground truth (so a reworded triple needs the second opinion applied again, which the cache makes free), and leaves the rows of records not drafted now as they are. Rows are ordered by pool position. The file is read by `common/common_helpers/second_opinion.py`; `drafted_by`, in the ground truth files, stays what it is: who first drafted the row. The file starts with its header only, and nothing but `--apply true` writes it. Commit it after applying, with `annotations/second_opinion_candidates.csv` (below; the commands: `docs/running_on_nasa_laptop.md`, *A second opinion on the ground truth*). Reading it stops (step 070 names the line) on a header with the counts' names of before 2026-11-01 (`runs`, `found_in_runs`, `found_exact_in_runs`; rename them as in this table), on a row without a model, on counts that aren't whole numbers with repeats ≥ 1 and 0 ≤ `found_exact_in_repeats` ≤ `found_in_repeats` ≤ `repeats`, and on two rows of one model for one triple.

A ground truth triple's **group** by the second opinion: *found* (a model found it in at least one repeat: a row's `found_in_repeats` is above 0), *not found* (found in none: every row's `found_in_repeats` is 0), *not checked* (no row: the record wasn't drafted, or the triple was reworded since).

## The draft's facts to review: `annotations/second_opinion_candidates.csv`

With `--apply true`, the draft's facts the ground truth lacks, of the tuning records, go to `annotations/second_opinion_candidates.csv` too, so that I can review each one against its record and the annotation guidelines. `second_opinion_matches.csv` lists them as well, but `outputs/` is never committed, so this file is their one home in Git (written by `helpers/second_opinion.py`, `apply_candidates`; read by `common/common_helpers/second_opinion.py`, `read_candidates`). One row per classed triple of a tuning record and per model, the union over the repeats:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `id`, `title` | The record and its title. |
| `subject`, `subject_class`, `predicate`, `object`, `object_class` | The draft's classed triple, as the first repeat that proposed it writes it. |
| `source_text` | Its source text, from the same repeat. |
| `model` | The model whose repeats proposed it. |
| `proposed_in_repeats` | In how many of its repeats a draft proposed it (a repeat stating it twice counts once). |
| `repeats` | How many of its repeats drafted the record. |
| `decision` | Mine, empty until I review the row (below). |

The `decision` is one of:

- `added`: I added the fact to the ground truth (in the annotation tool).
- `left out (<section>, rule <n>)`: a rule of the annotation guidelines leaves the fact out, named by its section's heading and its number as `annotations/guidelines.md` writes them, e.g. `left out (Writing a classed triple, rule 21)`.
- `already in the ground truth in other words`: a ground truth triple states the same fact in words the pairing can't match (*When a pair isn't the same fact*, in `pairs.md`).
- empty: not reviewed yet.

A decision is about the fact, so it is kept by the record's id and the classed triple (compared as step 070's exact pairs compare: `common/common_helpers/triples_io.py`, `classed_key`), whichever model's row it is on. Applying again with a model replaces that model's rows on the tuning records drafted now (with this run's counts), and each new row gets the decision already filled for the same record and classed triple, else an empty one. A row of that model with a decision filled whose classed triple this run no longer proposes (one I added to the ground truth since, which now pairs, among them) is kept as it was, so no decision is lost. Every other model's rows, and the rows of records not drafted now, are kept; a row of a record now in the held-out part is dropped. Rows are ordered by pool position, then the file's order. Held-out records' draft-only triples are never written (*Held-out records*, above).

Reading the file stops, naming the line, on a missing column, a row without an id or a model, counts that aren't whole numbers with 1 ≤ `proposed_in_repeats` ≤ `repeats`, a decision that isn't one of the above (a left-out decision naming a section and rule `annotations/guidelines.md` doesn't have among them), two rows of one model for one classed triple of a record, and two different decisions on one classed triple of a record. `--apply true` reads it, and `annotations/second_opinion.csv`, before its first paid call, so a mistake in either stops the run before it pays and before it writes anything. The file starts with its header only; nothing but `--apply true` writes it, and only the person reviewing fills `decision`. Step 070 doesn't read it.

## What step 070 does with it

Step 070 reads the file and gives, for each part, under *Metrics*, the subsection *By the second opinion*: for each group, its ground truth triples, [recall](../docs/terminology.md#7-evaluating-extraction-step-070) at both pair levels, and at the partial level its pairs, [precision](../docs/terminology.md#7-evaluating-extraction-step-070) and [F1](../docs/terminology.md#7-evaluating-extraction-step-070), with the formula (`070_evaluate/070_evaluate.md`, *By the second opinion*). Below that table, a second: for each model and number of repeats N, how many ground truth triples it found in 0, 1, ..., N repeats. It warns when a row names a classed triple that is no longer a ground truth triple of an evaluated record. The same numbers are in `metrics.json` (`parts.<part>.numbers.second_opinion` and `second_opinion_repeats`). A ground truth not yet checked by any second opinion gives no subsection.

## What it can't see

- **A fact both annotators missed** is still invisible: the second opinion lowers the number of such facts, it doesn't count them.
- **The same mistakes.** Two models of one maker, or two trained on similar data, miss similar facts; the default model is from another maker than 060's, but from the same maker as 050's. The report notes every ground truth triple whose `drafted_by` is the very model asked.
- **Words the pairing can't match**: a fact stated in different words on the two sides counts as not found (`pairs.md`, *Assumes and can't see*).
- **The schema view includes the record's own coined component classes**: the model sees, as 050 does, the component classes and patterns the finished tuning records use, these records among them; not their triples.
- **Repeats of one model aren't independent annotators**: they share its blind spots, so a fact found in none of 3 repeats is one this model misses on 3 answers, not one 3 annotators miss; and with LiteLLM's answer cache not bypassed, the repeats may be copies of one.
