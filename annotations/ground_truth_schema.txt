ENTITY CLASSES
--------------
Dataset           a set of observations, measurements, or samples
WebTool           a tool used over the web
Document          a written publication, or a set of them, named in words or by a reference key, e.g. NASA developed standards; LAWRENCEETAL2013
                  source: ground truth tuning #0, #5
Purpose           an aim something is meant to achieve
Mission           a named spaceflight effort
MissionPhase      a named phase of a Mission
Discipline        a field of science
Organization      a named institution or one of its named parts, not a Mission, e.g. NASA; OBPG
Instrument        a device that takes measurements
Spacecraft        a craft that operates in space
Time              a point or period on the calendar, which happens once, e.g. March 11, 2016 19:08:03; May 4, 2002; the first week of February 2003; 2015-10-22 to 2015-12-31
PhysicalQuantity  a property that can be measured, in general, whatever method measures it, e.g. sea surface temperature; snow cover
CelestialBody     a natural object in space
Version           a label that marks one release of something
SpatialExtent     the area the data cover, of the CelestialBody they are about: all of it (global) or part of it (e.g. the Arctic; the south pole of Mars)
ProcessingLevel   how far data have been processed from what the Instrument recorded, e.g. Level 2
Value             an amount, as a number with its unit or in words, e.g. 36 spectral bands; 5 minutes
Algorithm         a method applied to data to compute a result, e.g. a series of screens; a modified nonlinear SST algorithm
                  source: ground truth tuning #2
Identifier        a code that names one thing permanently, e.g. a DOI
                  source: ground truth tuning #2
SpatialRepresentation  how data are laid out over the ground: along the satellite's path, as observed (swath), or on a fixed map grid (gridded)
                       source: ground truth tuning #2
Country           a nation, e.g. U.S.; Argentina
                  source: ground truth tuning #3
TimeOfDay         a point or period in the day, which comes back every day, e.g. 2:39 PM; morning; nighttime
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Orbit             the path a Spacecraft travels around a CelestialBody
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
SatelliteFormation  a group of Spacecraft flying together in formation in orbit, e.g. the A-train
                    source: ground truth tuning #4
Channel           a range of wavelengths an Instrument measures separately, e.g. the 11 micron channel
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Phenomenon        something that happens in nature and can be observed, e.g. sun glint
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Grid              a fixed set of cells that data values are collected into, e.g. ISEAG
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
MapProjection     a way of drawing the curved Earth on a flat map, e.g. equidistant cylindrical
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
WebPage           a web address, written as the address itself, e.g. https://oceancolor.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/format/
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Team              a group of people without a name of its own, e.g. Peter Minnett and his team
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Task              a piece of work a Person, Team, or Organization does or is in charge of, e.g. quality flagging
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Role              a position a Person, Team, or Organization holds, what they are, e.g. the official archive for SST
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Variable          one named kind of value computed from an Instrument's measurements by one particular Algorithm, e.g. SST4; NDSI
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Person            one named human being, e.g. Peter Minnett
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
SpatialResolution  the amount of ground each value of the data covers, e.g. 500 m; 4.63 km
                   source: ground truth tuning #2, #4
TemporalResolution  the amount of time between one value of the data and the next, e.g. daily; weekly (8 day)
                    source: ground truth tuning #2, #3, #4

PREDICATES
----------
Each entry below reads subject -> object, and lists its patterns (subject class -> object class).
Every pattern the ground truth itself uses counts too (code reads them from the
ground truth files), so a new pattern is never added here. Use a predicate
wherever its meaning fits.
A line "only: Subject -> Object; ..." pins a predicate: it joins only those
entity classes ("any": any entity class), and the checks flag any other pattern.
