ENTITY CLASSES
--------------
Dataset           a set of observations, measurements, or samples
WebTool           a tool used over the web
Document          a written publication
Purpose           an aim something is meant to achieve
Mission           a named spaceflight effort
MissionPhase      a named phase of a mission
Discipline        a field of science
Organization      an institution or one of its parts; not a mission
Instrument        a device that takes measurements
Spacecraft        a craft that operates in space
TimeSpan          a period with a start and an end
PhysicalQuantity  a property that can be measured
CelestialBody     a natural object in space
Version           a label marking one release of something
SpatialExtent     the area the data cover, of the body they are about: all of it (global) or part of it (e.g. the Arctic, or the south pole of Mars)
ProcessingLevel   how far data have been processed from what the instrument recorded, e.g. Level 2
Value             a number with its unit, e.g. 500 m
Algorithm         a method applied to data to compute a result, e.g. a series of screens, or a modified nonlinear SST algorithm
                  source: ground truth tuning #2
Identifier        a code that names one thing for good, e.g. a DOI
                  source: ground truth tuning #2
SpatialRepresentation  how data are laid out over the ground: either along the satellite's path, as observed (swath), or on a fixed map grid (gridded)
                       source: ground truth tuning #2
TemporalResolution  how often data give a value, e.g. daily, weekly, monthly
                    source: ground truth tuning #2, #3
Country           a nation, e.g. U.S., Argentina
                  source: ground truth tuning #3
TimeOfDay         a part of the day of the body observed, when observations were made, e.g. daytime, nighttime
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Date              one day in the calendar, e.g. May 4, 2002
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Orbit             the path a spacecraft travels around a body
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
SatelliteFormation  a group of satellites flying together in formation, e.g. the A-train
                    source: ground truth tuning #4
Channel           a range of wavelengths an instrument measures separately, e.g. the 11 and 12 micron channels
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Phenomenon        something that happens in nature and can be observed, e.g. sun glint
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Grid              a fixed set of cells that data values are collected into, e.g. ISEAG
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
MapProjection     a way of drawing the curved Earth on a flat map, e.g. equidistant cylindrical
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
WebPage           a page on the web, given by its address
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Team              a group of people who work together, e.g. Peter Minnett and his team
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Task              a piece of work someone is in charge of
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Role              a named job an organization or person holds, e.g. an official archive
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Retrieval         an estimate of a physical quantity, computed from an instrument's measurements by one particular method, e.g. SST4
                  source: ground truth tuning #4
Person            one named human being, e.g. Peter Minnett
                  source: ground truth tuning #4

PREDICATES
----------
Each entry below reads subject -> object, and lists its patterns (subject class -> object class).
Every pattern the ground truth itself uses counts too (code reads them from the
ground truth files), so a new pattern is never added here. Use a predicate
wherever its meaning fits.
A line "only: Subject -> Object; ..." pins a predicate: it joins only those
entity classes ("any": any entity class), and the checks flag any other pattern.
