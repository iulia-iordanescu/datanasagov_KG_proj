# annotations/rules_model_differences.txt -- every way in which the model's copy of the rules differs from the
# annotation guidelines, each with its reason.
#
# The rules have one home, annotations/guidelines.md. The model's copy, common/common_prompts/extraction_rules.txt,
# is built from it by `py helpers/build_rules.py` (common/common_helpers/model_rules.py says how), never edited by
# hand: a test fails when it differs from what the build gives. The build takes the guidelines' words as they are,
# leaves out the guidelines' examples (they quote ground truth records, which no prompt may), takes out each ground
# truth schema name in parentheses (the rules state each fact by its meaning; the model uses the schema its task
# names), and then applies the entries below. A difference that is not an entry here cannot exist.
#
# An entry starts with a line
#
#   @@ kind | where | reason
#
# and its body follows, up to the next "@@" line. Kinds:
#   preamble   text the model's copy starts with.
#   replace    the guidelines' words (as plain text: no links, no * or `), a line "=>", then the model's words;
#              nothing after "=>" leaves the words out of the model's copy. The guidelines' words must be found
#              exactly once.
#   map        lines "word => word": a word replaced everywhere in the model's copy.
#   examples   "where" is "rule N" or "rule N / Part title"; each "- " line is an invented example, placed after
#              that rule's text or that part (no reason needed: every example of the model's copy is invented,
#              since the guidelines' examples quote ground truth records).
#   also       "- " items of the model's section ALSO, after the rules.
# An item's continuation lines are indented.

- "The 4 and 8 GHz bands": one band can't be both 4 GHz and 8 GHz, so two triples.

@@ examples | rule 22 / Exception: a collective "and"
- "smoothed with a spline approach using both upper and lower knots" gives one method, "spline using
  both upper and lower knots".
- "Designed to keep continuity of rainfall estimates from older and newer gauges" gives one purpose,
  "keep continuity of rainfall estimates from older and newer gauges".
- "the ratio of the red and near-infrared reflectances" gives one object, "ratio of the red and
  near-infrared reflectances".
- "merged Sentinel and Landsat" splits, since the data come from each.

@@ examples | rule 23
- "Generated by the Tidewater Data Group (TDG)" ... "acquires data from the TDG": both triples use
  "Tidewater Data Group (TDG)".
- "7km" in the title, "7.41 km" in the notes: "7.41 km". "T3" in the title, "Tier 3" in the notes:
  "Tier 3".
- A title "TERN Seasonal Lake Ice Cover" and notes "TERN maps lake ice cover over open water": neither
  contains the other, and only the notes' sentence states the measurement with words for it ("maps"),
  so its measurements are of "lake ice cover over open water".

@@ examples | rule 23 / Type words
- "The TERN Project was ..." and later "the TERN project ended" give "TERN" in every triple.
- "the Kestrel low gain sensor" stays whole, since "Kestrel low gain" isn't a name.
- "a spline approach" gives "spline", a name; "a modified two-band ice algorithm" stays whole, since
  "modified two-band ice" isn't a name.
- "derived from the IC2 swath product" gives "IC2"; "the CAL data" stays whole; "described on the IC2
  product page" gives the document "IC2 product page", whole, since "IC2" alone is the name of the
  product, not of its page.

@@ examples | rule 24
- "this Tern Lake Ice Extent product, used to make the Tern Lake Albedo product" gives the record's
  dataset produced using "Tern", with measurements of "Lake Ice Extent"; "Tern Lake Albedo" stays whole,
  the name of another product.

@@ examples | rule 24 / The record's dataset's own values
- A record titled "Orca Lake Ice Fraction Daily L3" that says "The fractions are computed from the Orca
  swath data with a spline fit" and "The error values hold the standard deviation of the residuals"
  gives the record's dataset computed from "Orca swath data" and produced using "spline fit", and
  "error values" produced using "standard deviation".
- "KX31 is the ice roughness parameter for band K4. The albedo values for band K4 are made from the
  roughness parameter and two other parameters" gives "albedo values for band K4" computed from the
  record's dataset, since the roughness parameter is the record's dataset's own values and the record
  describes no step that made it.

@@ examples | rule 24 / One step after another
- A record says its dataset holds "ice cover detected from the IWI using a threshold test", and that
  "the IWI is derived from reflectance data": two steps. Step 1: IWI = ?(reflectance data); the method
  isn't named, so only "IWI" computed from "reflectance data". Step 2: the record's dataset = threshold
  test(IWI): the record's dataset computed from "IWI", and produced using "threshold test"; its
  measurements are of "ice cover", without the words for the input and the method.

@@ examples | rule 24 / Words that name no method
- "The count totals are kept as 10 s sums of counts" gives the record's dataset produced using "sums"
  (and the compositing period "10 s", rule 18).
- "the counts were detrended using the TERN background map" gives "detrended" no "produced using"
  triple, and the counts computed from the map.
- "The dataset's reflectances were corrected, then composited using the Tern algorithm" gives the
  record's dataset produced using "Tern"; "corrected" names no method, so it gives no "produced using"
  triple. In "corrected reflectances", "corrected" stays in the instance.
- "stray striping has been removed from the data set" gives the record's dataset cleaned of "stray
  striping"; "glint corrected reflectances" gives the record's dataset cleaned of "glint", and "glint
  corrected reflectances" stays whole.

@@ examples | rule 24 / Input or method
- "The IWI is derived from reflectance data" makes "reflectance data" an input: "IWI" computed from
  "reflectance data". "Tern", above, is a method: it is applied again to other data.
- "made weekly from 30 days of data" gives the temporal resolution "weekly" and the compositing period
  "30 days", and no computed from.

@@ also | ALSO | The guidelines say this under Finishing a record, for the person; the model's copy has no such section.
- Connect your triples: every triple should link, through the others, to the record's title. If one
  doesn't, look for the fact the record states that connects it, and never invent one. When the record
  states no such fact, or that fact can't be written with the predicates you may use, keep the triple
  anyway (rule 21).
